Introduction to Portrait Lighting Correction

Every photographer knows the frustration of capturing a shot with great emotion and expression, only to find the lighting conditions fell short. Facial shadows may be too harsh, the background underexposed, or the main subject lost in the dark. Traditional editing using adjustment brushes or gradients can be time-consuming and requires meticulous masking. That is where artificial intelligence comes to the rescue. In this article, you will learn how to use ai relight in luminar neo to restore tonal balance in just a few minutes without destroying the natural skin texture.

What is the AI Relight Tool in Luminar Neo?

The AI Relight tool analyzes a two-dimensional image and estimates its depth using neural network algorithms. This allows the software to separate the foreground from the background and recognize human subjects. Unlike a traditional exposure slider that affects the entire frame uniformly, AI Relight gives you independent control over the light falling on the subject versus the environment. This is a crucial feature for portrait photographers who want to guide the viewer's eye exactly where it needs to be.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Use AI Relight

The lighting correction process in Luminar Neo is intuitive, yet it requires an understanding of individual sliders. Follow these steps to properly correct an underexposed portrait:

  • Step 1: Launch Luminar Neo and import your chosen portrait in RAW or JPEG format into the Catalog.
  • Step 2: Go to the "Edit" tab in the upper right corner of the screen to access the retouching tool panel.

Once you are in the editor, you need to locate the correct tool section:

  • Step 3: Under the "Portrait" section, find the tool named "AI Relight". Click it to expand the control panel.
  • Step 4: Pay attention to the two main brightness sliders: "Brightness Near" and "Brightness Far". The "Near" slider affects the photographed person, while the "Far" slider affects the background behind them.

Move the "Brightness Near" slider to the right to gradually brighten the subject. You will notice that the algorithm automatically avoids areas that were already correctly exposed, protecting them from clipping.

  • Step 5: Adjust the "Brightness Far" slider if you want to brighten or darken the background to better isolate your subject from the surroundings.

Another powerful element of this tool is color temperature control:

  • Step 6: Use the "Warmth Near" and "Warmth Far" sliders. These allow you to simulate warm golden hour light or cool studio lighting, helping to harmoniously blend the subject into the background environment.
  • Step 7: Utilize advanced depth controls, such as the "Depth" slider, to precisely tell the software where the boundary lies between the model and the background if the initial AI analysis requires fine-tuning.

Honest Look at Program Limitations

Although artificial intelligence technology in Luminar Neo is advanced, it is important to understand its natural boundaries. AI Relight cannot conjure details where the source file suffers from digital quantization noise or complete data loss (pure black patches with no color information). If an image is severely underexposed and shadows are completely flat, lifting them with AI may amplify digital noise and compression artifacts. In such cases, it is recommended to use AI denoising tools first before proceeding with lighting correction. Additionally, for very complex hairstyles or clothing with fringes, the AI-generated mask might require manual refinement using a mask brush.
