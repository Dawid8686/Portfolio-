Introduction to Red-Eye Correction

The red-eye effect is one of the most common issues in portrait photography. It typically occurs when a camera's built-in flash reflects off the subject's retina in low-light conditions. Although modern cameras and smartphones handle this phenomenon increasingly well, traditional flash photography sessions can still be problematic.

For beginner photographers, fixing such flaws can sometimes be frustrating. Fortunately, modern photo editing software offers dedicated tools that solve this problem in just a few seconds. In this article, you will learn how to remove red eye in luminar neo using the program's simple features.

Preparing Your Photo for Editing

Before taking control of portrait details, perform a few basic preparatory steps:

  • Launch Luminar Neo and import your chosen photo in RAW or JPEG format.
  • Go to the Edit tab in the upper right panel.
  • Zoom in on the eye area (using keyboard shortcuts or the zoom tool) to clearly see the iris and pupil.

Step-by-Step: How to Remove Red Eye in Luminar Neo

Luminar Neo features a dedicated tool in the portrait section designed specifically for this task. Here is the step-by-step procedure:

Step 1: Open Portrait Tools

In the edit panel, find the Portrait section. Expand it to see the list of available sliders and features responsible for improving skin, eyes, lips, and facial structure.

Step 2: Find the Eye-Related Section

Inside the portrait section, look for the submenu titled Eyes. This is where you can modify brightness, contrast, and correct the coloration of this facial area.

Step 3: Use the Red Eye Removal Tool

Look for the feature labeled Red Eye Removal. Once activated, your mouse cursor will change into a brush or crosshair.

Step 4: Select the Area

Click directly on the area of the pupil turned red. The program algorithm will analyze the pixels lit up by the flash and automatically replace the unwanted red with a natural, dark color.

Step 5: Manual Correction

If the automatic detection does not work perfectly (which can happen with unusual lighting), adjust the brush size and click again, or use the adjustment brush tools to manually darken the edges of the pupil.

Alternative Correction Methods for Difficult Cases

Sometimes the standard red-eye removal tool may not be enough—for example, when the light reflection is irregular. In such situations, you can rely on other program modules:

  • Use the Develop tool to locally lower exposure and saturation of the red channel using the Masking brush.
  • Use the Erase tool to get rid of unnatural reflections, although with eyes, this method must be used with extreme caution to avoid distorting anatomy.
  • Enhance the overall iris brightness using eye-whitening sliders in the portrait section, which naturally distracts from any imperfections.
