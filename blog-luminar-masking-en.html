What is new in Masking

The maker has redesigned selective editing on desktop to make it more intuitive, flexible and precise. The main changes:

  • Masking right where you edit. The tool is part of the editing flow itself.
  • Smarter selections. Less manual work when choosing an area.
  • More creative control. More ways to fine-tune the result.
  • Copy your edits. Carry your adjustments over to other photos and keep your own style.
  • Background Removal, refined. Faster cut-outs of a subject.

How to get started

  1. Download the beta from the manufacturer's site and try the new Masking on your own photos before buying anything.
  2. Start with one photo: select the subject, adjust light only on it, then clean up the background.
  3. When you like the result, copy the edits to the rest of the series.
  4. Only then pick a plan (below).

The redesign applies to the desktop version.

Using it for product photos

Selective editing helps when you want to brighten only the product, even out colour across a series or clean up the background. Copying edits saves time when many photos share one style. One rule: retouch the look, not the facts. Improve light and colour; do not hide flaws or change what the buyer will actually receive.
