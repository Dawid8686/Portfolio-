Luminar Prime as a one-time payment (€69): what stays forever and what ends in 2027

What changed

Customers now have two options: Prime on subscription or Prime as a one-time payment of €69. The second gives access to premium features without recurring payments.

What stays forever

All non-generative features unlocked through this upgrade remain available permanently. That is the main argument for the one-time payment: a predictable cost and no dependence on a subscription.

What has a deadline: 30 September 2027

Access to generative tools and the library remains available until 30 September 2027. After that date the manufacturer will discontinue these services, while previously unlocked non-generative features keep working.

Which features are non-generative, check in the plan description on the Skylum site. The manufacturer's description takes precedence over this post.

Who it suits (my view)

  • You mostly retouch, mask and colour-correct (e.g. product photos): a one-time payment gives permanent access to what you need.
  • You want to avoid recurring fees: a one-time €69 is easier to plan.
  • Your work relies on generative tools or the library: keep the 30 September 2027 date in mind and check how it applies to your plan.

Checklist before buying

  1. Download a trial or the beta and check whether the tools you use are non-generative.
  2. Note what you use daily (masks, background removal, copying edits).
  3. Compare with the Black Friday offers and, if you are a subscriber, with the All-in-One upgrade.
