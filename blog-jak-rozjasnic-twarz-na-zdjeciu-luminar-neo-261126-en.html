Introduction to Modern Portrait Retouching

Portrait editing often requires striking a delicate balance between the exposure of the overall frame and the illumination of the face itself. Traditional brush or vignetting methods can be time-consuming and rarely look 100% natural. Fortunately, modern graphic software offers intelligent algorithms that can recognize human features and precisely manipulate light without disturbing the rest of the composition. In this article, you will learn how to brighten face in Luminar Neo using dedicated artificial intelligence tools: Face AI and Portrait AI.

Luminar Neo has gained popularity due to its approach based on AI adjustment layers and masking. The program analyzes the content of the frame, which saves a lot of time. However, it is worth keeping in mind the software's limitations – AI algorithms sometimes struggle with highly complex lighting, harsh shadows cast by third objects, or complete darkness where skin details are invisible to the sensor. Understanding the mechanics of the program, however, allows you to achieve great results in almost any standard studio or outdoor scenario.

File Preparation and Initial Exposure

Before moving on to advanced portrait features, you must properly prepare your file (preferably a RAW format, which provides the greatest tonal range). The first step is working in the Develop module.

  • Import your photo into the Luminar Neo catalog and go to the Edit tab.
  • In the Develop section, correct the overall exposure, white balance, and contrast of the entire frame.
  • Pay attention to the Highlights and Shadows sliders. If the background is too bright, darken it slightly so the viewer's eye naturally wanders toward the center of the frame.
  • Avoid excessively brightening the entire photo just to make the face readable – we will use dedicated AI tools for that in the following steps.

Good preparation of the base allows Face AI algorithms to work on cleaner material, which directly translates to fewer digital noises and artifacts around skin edges.

Face AI – Precise Facial Features Brightening

Once the base exposure is ready, navigate to the Portrait section in the right sidebar. This is where the powerful Face AI tool is hidden, automatically detecting eyes, mouth, nose, and the overall facial contour.

To naturally brighten the face, follow these technical steps:

  • Expand the Face tab and locate the slider named „Face Light”.
  • Move the slider to the right very carefully – values between 15 and 30 are usually more than enough. A value that is too high will create an unnatural "mask" and separate the face from the rest of the body.
  • The algorithm automatically detects the head tilt angle and adjusts the lighting strength, simulating a virtual photographic reflector.
  • If you want to brighten specific areas, such as the eye area (usually underexposed by a cap brim or eyebrows), use the „Dark Circles Removal” slider, which delicately brightens these parts.

Keep in mind that limitations of this feature can include profiles with a strong side-head placement or extreme shadows – in such situations, AI may not accurately map the corners of the eyes or lips.

Portrait AI and Additional Skin Refinement

Simply brightening the skin can highlight its imperfections, pores, or uneven skin tone. Therefore, the next step is to use the Portrait AI tool, which takes care of skin texture and smoothness while preserving natural pores (the so-called non-plastic skin effect).

  • Go to the Skin section in the portrait panel.
  • Turn on the „Skin AI” option and adjust the „Amount” slider. To maintain realism, stick to low values (between 10 and 25).
  • Check the „Shine Removal” option to reduce unsightly light reflections on the forehead or nose, which often occur when the face is brightened more intensely.
  • Use the „Slim Face” tool extremely carefully – even a minimal change of 5 points can affect the recognizability of the portrayed person.

Combining Face Light and Skin AI allows you to achieve a clean, luminous portrait without resorting to complicated brush masking.

Masking and Local Adjustments in Luminar Neo

It often happens that AI automation needs correction. If the algorithm also brightened parts of clothing or hair, you must use the masking brush (Masking).

  • With the Face AI modification selected, click the mask icon (Brush).
  • Select the „Eraser” mode and paint over those areas of the background, clothing, or hair that should not be affected by the brightening effect.
  • You can also use the „Mask AI” feature, which allows you to select e.g. only skin or lips with one click, although for minor corrections, the traditional brush gives full control.

This is the technical moment when the program shows its flexibility, but also requires patience – precise cutting of the face from a jacket collar or dark hair is crucial for maintaining a professional look.

Exporting the Portrait in Highest Quality

Once you have achieved a satisfactory effect of a naturally brightened face, it is time for the proper export of the file to preserve details and the appropriate color space.

  • Click the export button in the top right corner of the Luminar Neo interface.
  • Choose the target format: JPEG works ideally for publishing on social media and blogs, while TIFF or full sRGB/AdobeRGB 16-bit is recommended if you plan professional printing.
  • Set Quality to 100% in case of JPEG format.
  • In the Sharpening section, select „Standard” or „High”, depending on whether the photo will be downscaled.
  • Specify the target save folder on your computer's hard drive and confirm the export process.

Summary and Best Practices

Brightening faces using Face AI and Portrait AI in Luminar Neo is a powerful technological shortcut that significantly shortens post-production time. The key to success is moderation – subtle slider values, attention to edge masking, and avoiding excessive skin smoothing allow you to maintain the natural character of the portrait. Remember that no AI can replace good lighting on the set, but these programs do a great job of saving underexposed frames and giving them a professional touch.
