Introduction to color management in Luminar Neo

Color editing is one of the most crucial stages of post-processing. It allows you to not only set the desired mood of a photo but also correct lighting imperfections or distract the viewer from unwanted elements. Luminar Neo offers advanced yet intuitive tools for working with color. In this article, you will learn how to change colors in Luminar Neo using two powerful features: the Color Transfer tool and the HSL panel within the Develop module.

Before diving in, it is fair to mention the software's limitations. Luminar Neo relies on AI algorithms and selective masks, which work great in most cases. However, with very complex edges, light reflections, or transparencies, automatic masking may require manual brush adjustments. Despite this, working with colors in this program is significantly faster than in traditional editors.

Method 1: Precise HSL (Hue, Saturation, Luminance) editing

The HSL panel is a classic, extremely powerful tool for controlling individual color channels. You can find it in the Develop edit module. It allows for independent modification of hue, saturation, and luminance across eight basic color ranges.

Step-by-step: Changing the hue of a specific object

Imagine you have a red car in your photo, and you want to change its color to an elegant blue. Here is how to do it:

  • Open your chosen photo in the Edit module in Luminar Neo.
  • Expand the Develop tool, then navigate to the HSL tab.
  • Select the Hue tab, which is responsible for shifting the color.
  • Click the color picker icon (eyedropper) and click directly on the red car in the photo so the program highlights the corresponding slider.
  • Move the slider left or right to shift the red hue toward orange or magenta until you achieve the desired blue shade.

Step-by-step: Adjusting saturation and luminance

Hue changes alone are rarely enough to make a color look natural. The newly created shade might be too vivid or too dark.

  • Switch to the Saturation tab in the HSL panel to control the intensity of the newly obtained color.
  • Decrease the saturation if the color stands out too much and looks unnatural.
  • Switch to the Luminance tab to brighten or darken the object. Sometimes changing a color from dark to bright requires boosting luminance to preserve shadow details.

Method 2: Advanced color matching with Color Transfer

The Color Transfer tool is a feature that allows you to apply the color palette from one photo to another. If you want a specific color mood, such as a cinematic teal and orange palette, this is the fastest way to achieve it.

How Color Transfer works in practice

This feature analyzes the tonal distribution of the source (reference) image and applies it to your edited photo. You do not need to manually manipulate dozens of sliders – artificial intelligence does it for you.

  • Go to the Presets tab or find the Color Transfer tool in the Creative tools section, depending on your software version.
  • Select one of the reference photos from the program's library or upload your own graphic file whose color palette you like.
  • Confirm your selection and wait a moment for the algorithm to apply the color profile to your photograph.
  • Use the Opacity slider to reduce the intensity of the effect if it is too aggressive and ruins the natural look of the photo.

Masking and local adjustments for full control

One of the biggest challenges when changing colors is that the modification often affects other elements in the photo with a similar hue (e.g., changing a car's red color also alters a person's shirt in the background). To prevent this, Luminar Neo offers masking tools.

How to mask a color change?

After making adjustments in the HSL panel or another editing tool:

  • Click the mask icon (brush) located next to the respective editing tool.
  • Select the Brush option.
  • Paint over the areas that the color change should not affect (using the Erase mode), or vice versa – apply the mask only to the object of interest.
  • You can also use the AI masking feature if the program successfully detects the object (e.g., people, sky, or buildings).

Keep in mind that manual masking requires precision, especially with complex edges. If you want to learn more about professional retouching and the capabilities of this program, check out Luminar Neo and test it on your own photographs.

Conclusion

Color editing in Luminar Neo offers immense possibilities for both beginners and advanced creators. Knowing the HSL panel allows for quick and controlled recoloring of objects, while Color Transfer makes it easy to impart a unique, artistic mood to your photos. Remember, however, that the key to natural-looking photographs is moderation and precise masking of areas that should remain unchanged.
