What All-in-One covers

The plan extends access across the entire Luminar ecosystem: the desktop, mobile and web apps, plus an extensive library of courses and creative assets.

Price and how it is calculated

  • Yearly plan price: €119.
  • Existing subscribers receive a personalised, prorated upgrade price based on their current plan and its remaining value.
  • You see your price after logging in to your Skylum account.

Rules worth knowing

  • The new plan is available on a yearly basis only.
  • Existing monthly and multi-year subscriptions transition to the annual model.
  • You can also keep your current subscription, with access limited to your current platform.
