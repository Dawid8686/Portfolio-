Introduction to Haze-Free Landscape Photography

Landscape photography comes with many challenges, and one of the toughest to overcome is natural mist, haze, and airborne dust. While sometimes atmospheric haze adds mood, it often flattens images, destroying contrast and natural colors. Traditional editing required tedious masking and tone curves. Fortunately, modern software offers AI-driven solutions. In this article, you will learn how to remove haze in luminar neo using two powerful tools: Enhance AI and Atmosphere AI.

Luminar Neo gained popularity thanks to intuitive algorithms that analyze image content, allowing precise intervention without ruining original details. Before you start, remember the program's limitations. AI handles standard atmospheric haze well, but it cannot recreate details in areas completely blocked by thick clouds or severely blown-out highlights.

Preparing RAW Files for Luminar Neo

Before applying artificial intelligence features, perform basic preliminary adjustments. Working with RAW files yields the best results due to high dynamic range.

  • Import your landscape photo into the Luminar Neo library.
  • Go to the Edit tab located on the right side of the interface.
  • In the Develop tool section, correct blown highlights and lift shadows slightly if needed.
  • Make sure the horizon is straight – AI algorithms work most accurately on correctly cropped images.

Step 1: Enhance AI Tool and Accent AI

The fastest way to improve clarity is the Enhance AI tool. You will find it at the top of the tools list in the edit panel. This is where the core automatic contrast and exposure correction happens.

Expand Enhance AI and locate the Accent AI slider. It intelligently analyzes tones, saturation, light, and shadows across the entire frame. When you drag it to the right:

    The software automatically recognizes hazy areas and increases micro-contrast there.

    You will notice distant mountains or trees becoming clearer, and the sky gaining depth.

    Unlike traditional contrast sliders, Accent AI does not drastically blow out highlights or completely crush shadows.

For most light hazes, setting the slider between 30 and 50 is enough to restore a natural look. Do not overdo it, as aggressive values can create unnatural halos around horizon lines.

Step 2: Removing Haze with Sky Enhancer AI

Inside the Enhance AI section, you will also find the Sky Enhancer AI slider. Although its name implies working only with the sky, this algorithm often helps reduce light haze hanging right above the horizon.

Dragging this slider to the right darkens blue skies and removes the glare typical of misty mornings. If your shot consists largely of sky and distant scenery, this tool works synergistically with Accent AI to speed up the editing process.

Step 3: Atmosphere AI for Advanced Haze Control

Sometimes haze is an intentional composition element but sits in the wrong spot, or it is too dense to clear with contrast sliders alone. In such cases, the Atmosphere AI tool under the Creative section comes to the rescue.

While Atmosphere AI is primarily meant for adding fog, rain, or sun rays, it includes a built-in depth detection mechanism. This means the software knows what is in the foreground and what is in the background.

  • Select Atmosphere AI from the Creative menu.
  • Choose a fog type from the dropdown list (e.g., Layered Fog).
  • Use the Amount slider to adjust visibility.
  • Use the Depth slider to control where the effect appears in the scene. Shifting it toward the background helps neutralize unwanted depth haze by adding a clean layer that organizes spatial planes.

Remember that Atmosphere AI is a helper tool. If you need fully professional software for advanced landscape editing, check out the official Luminar Neo website and explore the full potential of this software.

Step 4: Masking and Fine-Tuning Details

Even the best AI can misjudge a scene. Haze can be uneven and overlap foreground objects that should remain sharp and saturated.

To fix algorithm errors:

  • Use the Brush Mask available under every tool.
  • Lower the brush opacity and gently paint over areas where the haze removal was too strong or too weak.
  • Use the Structure AI tool to selectively bring back details on background rocks or buildings that still look soft after clearing the haze.

Summary and Software Limitations

Removing haze in Luminar Neo is fast and intuitive thanks to Accent AI and depth-aware Atmosphere AI. The software saves dozens of minutes compared to traditional brush and luminosity mask methods.

Keep limitations in mind. Luminar Neo relies on an AI-generated depth map. In complex scenes—like a dense forest with numerous branches covered in mist—the algorithm might require manual brush corrections. Always inspect results at 100% zoom to ensure object edges remain clean and natural.
